Maddalena Grignani

PhD Candidate in Economics

Pompeu Fabra University, Barcelona

I am an applied microeconomist working in labor and health economics, with a secondary interest in the economics of education. My research examines how public policies, notably sex education and access to healthcare, shape women's fertility, careers, and exposure to violence. My job market paper estimates the long-run effects of sex education on women's labor market outcomes and gender norms. A second line of work uses survey experiments to investigate how individuals form beliefs about public institutions and whether lost trust can be restored.

Advisor: Libertad González.
