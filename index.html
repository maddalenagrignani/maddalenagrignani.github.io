Maddalena Grignani

PhD Student in Economics

Pompeu Fabra University, Barcelona

My research lies at the intersection of education and health economics, with a focus on how policies and institutions shape individual behavior and social norms. My main line of work examines the long-run effects of sex education on women's labor force participation and fertility, on social norms, and on intimate partner violence. I also study how access to healthcare affects fertility decisions, and how individuals form and update their beliefs about public institutions.

Advisor: Libertad González.
